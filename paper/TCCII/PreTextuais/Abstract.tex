\begin{resumo}[Abstract]
 \begin{otherlanguage*}{english}
The exponential growth of video streaming imposes critical challenges on Content Delivery Networks (CDNs), particularly regarding efficient load balancing among heterogeneous servers.
%
Conventional strategies such as \textit{Round-Robin} and Random Selection ignore the actual resource state of servers, resulting in overload of limited nodes and degradation of Quality of Experience (QoE).
%
This work proposes, implements, and validates a probabilistic load balancing algorithm based on RAM memory consumption and disk read rate monitoring, incorporating stale load information interpretation mechanisms adapted from the \textit{Interpreting Stale Load Information} method.
%
The solution was implemented in a complete architecture with MPEG-DASH servers, \textit{Rust}-based load balancer, MQTT communication, and \textit{Docker}-virtualized environment.
%
Experiments across four scenarios — varying concurrency (100/1000 users) and heterogeneity of resource availability per server — demonstrated latency reductions up to 85.7\% and complete elimination of stalls in heterogeneous environments under high load, validating the superiority of the proposed algorithm.
